Listening Practice Guide — K.E.Y.

K
Keywords
Write short notes only: names, dates, places, numbers and repeated words.
E
Expect Distractors
The first answer you hear may change. Wait for correction words like but, actually, or instead.
Y
Yield Final Answer
Choose the answer after listening to the full idea, not just one matching word.
Before submitting: check every question has an answer and compare it with your keyword notes.
