What Listening 800/1 tests

What MUET Listening Tests

  • 5 parts, 30 questions, approximately 50 minutes.
  • Each part plays once. You cannot replay the audio in the real exam.
  • Question types: note completion, multiple choice, matching, short answer.
  • The audio includes conversations, announcements, talks and discussions.
2
Tips & Tricks
Keyword and distractor strategy
▾
KEYWORD STRATEGY

Write 2–3 keywords per blank — not full sentences. If you miss a word, leave it and move on. Complete your notes after the audio ends.

DISTRACTOR WATCH

In real MUET audio, the first option mentioned is often corrected or changed by the speaker. Listen for signal words: but, however, actually, no — I mean, in fact. The corrected answer is almost always the right one.

Listening Note Example

Question: The meeting is on ___.
Poor note: "The lecturer said the meeting was moved from Thursday to Friday afternoon at two thirty pm in the science block."
Good note: "Fri / 2.30pm / sci block"

Short keywords save time and keep your attention on listening.

Sample Script (Short Announcement)

"Good morning students. I would like to remind you that the library will be closed this Thursday for maintenance. Actually — sorry, I made an error. The library will be closed on Friday, not Thursday. Opening will resume on Monday morning as usual."

Sample MCQ

When will the library be closed?

Answer: B — Friday.
The speaker first says Thursday, then corrects it to Friday. This is a classic distractor — the first option mentioned is wrong. Signal word: "Actually — sorry, I made an error."
