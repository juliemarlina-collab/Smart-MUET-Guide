Tips & Tricks
Strategies before you practise
▾
TASK A — PREP

Write 3–4 keywords during prep time — not full sentences. Start speaking immediately when prep ends. State your point in the first sentence.

TASK B — GROUP

Refer to others by name. Ask questions: "What do you think, [name]?" Help reach consensus in the last 2 minutes.

Quick Reminders

🎯One strong reason beats three weak ones
🇲🇾Use Malaysian examples — PTPTN, campus life, EPU
🚫Avoid fillers: um, uh, like, you know — pause instead
🔁Close by restating your point — second P in PREP

Sample Topic: Salary as reason for choosing a job

P — PointI believe salary is the most important reason when choosing a job because it directly determines financial security.
R — ReasonA good salary allows a graduate to repay education loans, cover living expenses and begin saving — all at once.
E — ExampleMany Malaysian graduates with PTPTN loans must prioritise salary because repayment starts six months after graduation. A salary below RM2,000 in KL makes this extremely difficult.
P — Point AgainTherefore, salary is the most critical factor as it forms the foundation of financial independence.
EXAMINER NOTE

Direct answer · one clear reason · specific local example · strong closing. About 90 seconds. No fillers.
