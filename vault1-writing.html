What MUET Writing Tests

  • Task 1: Email or letter. Respond to all bullet points. Use appropriate formal or semi-formal tone. Minimum ~150 words. Time: 25 minutes.
  • Task 2: Essay. Discursive or argumentative. State a clear thesis. Use PEEL paragraphs. Minimum ~350 words. Time: 50 minutes.
  • Both tasks are marked on: task fulfilment, organisation, language accuracy, vocabulary range and style.
  • Total: 90 marks (25% of MUET).
