Tips & Tricks
Strategies before you practise
▾
TASK A STRATEGY

Always use PREP: Point → Reason → Example → Point again. Write 3–4 keywords during prep time, not full sentences. Start speaking the moment prep time ends — do not wait.

TASK B STRATEGY

Task B is a group discussion, not a lecture. Refer to other candidates by name. Ask questions: "What do you think, Aisyah?". Help the group reach a consensus in the final minute.

Top 5 Speaking Tips

  1. State your main point in the first sentence. Examiners decide quickly.
  2. Give one strong reason, not three weak ones.
  3. Use a specific example — Malaysian context works well (e.g., PTPTN, EPU, JPA).
  4. Avoid fillers: um, uh, like, you know. Pause briefly instead.
  5. Close by restating your point — this is the second P in PREP.

Sample Task A Question

Do you think students should work part-time while studying? Give your opinion.

Sample PREP Response Band 4.0

P — Point I believe students should work part-time while studying because it builds real-world skills.
R — Reason Working part-time teaches students time management, financial responsibility and communication — skills that are hard to learn in a classroom alone.
E — Example For example, a friend of mine worked as a barista during her diploma. She improved her English significantly by interacting with customers daily, and she saved enough to reduce her PTPTN loan.
P — Point Again Therefore, I firmly believe part-time work enriches a student's overall development, provided it does not interfere with academic commitments.
EXAMINER NOTE

This response scores well because it answers directly, gives one clear reason, uses a specific example and closes with a restatement. No filler words. Took approximately 90 seconds.

Sample Task B Interaction Phrases

I agree with what you said, but I would like to add that...
[Name], what is your opinion on this?
Can we agree that the most important factor is...?
I think we should conclude that... since most of us agree on this point.
