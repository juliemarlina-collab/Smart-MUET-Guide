Candidate A

Individual Presentation · Part 1

📋 Instructions:

Situation: Students prepare for exams in different ways. What are some of the ways?

Your Task: You have to give a presentation to your class. Talk about studying in groups.

Preparation Time 2 minutes
Speaking Time 2 minutes

What to include:

Prepare your presentation about studying in groups. Think about:

  • Benefits of group study
  • How to organize a study group
  • What makes effective group study
  • Examples from your experience

💡 Tips for Studying in Groups:

Opening:
I think studying in groups is very effective because... / In my opinion, group study helps students because...
Main Points:
Firstly, group study encourages discussion... / Secondly, students can share different perspectives... / Another benefit is that we can help each other...
Conclusion:
In conclusion, group study is a great way to... / To sum up, I believe that studying together...

📝 Sample Answer:

I think studying in groups is very effective because it helps students understand difficult topics better. First, when students study together, they can discuss different ideas and perspectives. This helps them see the material from different angles. Second, group study allows students to teach each other, which makes learning more enjoyable. When I study with my friends, they can explain things I don't understand, and I can help them too.

Another benefit is that group study keeps us motivated. If we study alone, we might feel tired or bored, but with friends, studying becomes more fun and interesting. Finally, group study helps develop important skills like communication and teamwork. These skills are very useful for exams and for our future jobs.

In conclusion, I believe that studying in groups is an excellent way to prepare for exams. It makes learning easier, more enjoyable, and more effective.

Preparation Time 2 minutes

What to include:

Prepare your presentation about studying in a library. Think about:

  • Advantages of library study
  • Quiet and focused environment
  • Available resources
  • Why it's effective for exam prep

💡 Tips for Studying in a Library:

Opening:
Studying in a library is one of the best ways to prepare for exams... / I think the library is an excellent place to study because...
Main Points:
First, the library provides a quiet and focused environment... / Second, there are many resources available such as books and computers... / Additionally, fewer distractions help students concentrate better...

📝 Sample Answer:

I believe that studying in a library is one of the best ways to prepare for exams. The main reason is that the library provides a quiet and focused environment where students can concentrate fully on their studies. Unlike studying at home, there are fewer distractions.

Another advantage is that the library has many resources available, including reference books, journals, and computers with internet access. Students can find almost any information they need. The professional atmosphere also encourages serious studying. When you see other students studying hard, it motivates you to work harder too.

Additionally, librarians are available to help students find resources and answer questions. This support is very valuable when preparing for important exams. Finally, studying in a library helps develop good study habits that can be useful throughout life.

In conclusion, I strongly recommend studying in a library for exam preparation because of its quiet environment, available resources, and professional atmosphere.

Opening:
Extra classes are very helpful for exam preparation... / Attending additional tuition classes has many benefits for students...
Main Points:
The most important benefit is personalized attention from teachers... / Extra classes allow teachers to focus on students' weak areas... / Students receive guidance specifically tailored to their needs...

📝 Sample Answer:

Attending extra classes is an excellent way to prepare for exams. The main advantage is that students receive personalized attention from experienced teachers. In regular classes, teachers might not have enough time to help every student, but in extra classes, teachers can focus on individual student needs.

Another benefit is that extra classes allow teachers to focus specifically on areas where students struggle. If a student finds certain topics difficult, the teacher can explain those topics in more detail and provide extra practice. This targeted approach is much more effective than general studying.

Extra classes also help build confidence. When students understand difficult concepts better, they feel more confident during the actual exam. Additionally, teachers in extra classes often share exam tips and strategies that help students perform better.

Finally, extra classes provide structured learning, which is important for exam success. In conclusion, I believe extra classes are very valuable for effective exam preparation and improving academic performance.

Candidate D

Individual Presentation · Part 1

📋 Instructions:

Situation: Students prepare for exams in different ways. What are some of the ways?

Your Task: You have to give a presentation to your class. Talk about doing past year exam questions.

Preparation Time 2 minutes
Speaking Time 2 minutes

What to include:

Prepare your presentation about practicing with past exam questions. Think about:

  • Understanding exam format
  • Time management practice
  • Identifying common question patterns
  • Building exam confidence

💡 Tips for Past Year Questions:

Opening:
Practicing past exam questions is one of the most effective study methods... / I think working with previous exam papers is essential for good preparation...
Main Points:
First, it helps students understand the exam format... / Second, practicing under timed conditions improves time management... / Additionally, students can identify question patterns and trends...

📝 Sample Answer:

Practicing past year exam questions is one of the most important methods for exam preparation. First, it helps students understand the exact format of the exam. By working with real past papers, students know what to expect and can prepare specifically for that format.

Second, practicing past questions helps improve time management skills. When students work under timed conditions similar to the actual exam, they learn to manage their time better. This practice reduces anxiety on the actual exam day.

Another important benefit is that students can identify common question patterns and topics that frequently appear. By analyzing past papers, students can focus their study on important areas. This targeted approach makes study time more effective.

Additionally, working with past exam papers builds confidence. When students have practiced many similar questions, they feel more prepared and less nervous. They know they can handle the questions that will appear.

In conclusion, practicing past year exam questions is essential for successful exam preparation because it familiarizes students with format, improves time management, and builds confidence.

📢 Part 2: Group Discussion

After completing Part 1, practice this group discussion task with classmates or friends.

⏱️ Timing:
3 minutes preparation · 8-12 minutes discussion
📋 Situation:
Studying abroad is challenging. In your group, discuss how it is challenging.
🎯 Your task:
In your discussion, you may support or oppose the other test takers' views. At the end of the discussion, try to decide on THE MAIN REASON why studying abroad is challenging.
💡 Mind Map: Challenges of Studying Abroad
Why is studying abroad challenging?
🏠 Homesick
👥 Lack of friends
💬 Language problem
🌍 Cultural differences
💰 Cost of living
❓ Other ideas?

💬 Group Discussion Tips:

  • Support or oppose other speakers' opinions
  • Give reasons for your ideas
  • Listen to others and respond
  • Work together to reach a conclusion
  • Decide THE MAIN REASON by the end
