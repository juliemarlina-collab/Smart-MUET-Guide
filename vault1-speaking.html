Understand PREP
How to Structure Your Answer
▼
P
Point — State your stand
Begin with one clear opinion. Example: “I believe studying in groups is effective because…”
Start strong. The examiner listens to your opening sentence first.
R
Reason — Explain why
Use: because, the reason is, this is because, due to the fact that
Give one strong reason — do not list many weak ones.
E
Example — Malaysian context
Use: for example, for instance, a clear example of this is…
Local examples score better: school experience, friends, study methods.
P
Point Again — Close confidently
Restate your stand: “Therefore, I strongly believe that…”
This signals the examiner that you have completed your response.

SAMPLE TOPIC: STUDYING IN GROUPS
P
Point
R
Reason
E
Example
P
Point Again
P — I believe studying in groups is very effective because it helps students understand difficult topics better.

R — This is because when students study together, they can discuss different ideas and perspectives. They can teach each other, which makes learning more enjoyable.

E — For example, when I study with my friends, I can explain things they don't understand, and they can help me too. Group study keeps us motivated because with friends, studying becomes more fun and interesting.

P — Therefore, I strongly believe that studying in groups is an excellent way to prepare for exams because it makes learning easier, more enjoyable, and more effective.
✍️ Examiner note: Direct answer · one clear reason · specific relatable example · strong closing. About 90 seconds.

Useful Speaking Phrases
Ready-Made Phrases for PREP
▼
💭 STATING YOUR POINT (P)
“I believe that...” · “In my opinion...” · “I strongly feel that...”
“From my perspective...” · “I would argue that...”
💡 GIVING YOUR REASON (R)
“This is because...” · “The reason is...” · “Due to the fact that...”
“This can be attributed to...” · “One major reason is...”
🏠 GIVING AN EXAMPLE (E)
“For example...” · “For instance...” · “A clear example of this is...”
“From my own experience...” · “To illustrate this point...”
✅ RESTATING YOUR POINT (P)
“Therefore, I strongly believe that...” · “In conclusion...”
“To sum up...” · “This shows that...”
