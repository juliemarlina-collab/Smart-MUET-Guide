Tips & Tricks
Question-First Strategy
▾
QUESTION-FIRST

Always read the questions first. Underline keywords. Then scan the passage for those keywords — do not read every word.

Know Your Question Type

Direct retrieval — answer is almost word-for-word in the text
Paraphrase — same meaning, different words
Inference — implied by the text, read between the lines
AVOID DISTRACTORS

Options that use the same words as the passage but different meaning. Always check if the idea matches, not just the words.

Sample Explanation
How answers are explained
▾
Sample passage: Despite the increasing availability of electric vehicles, many Malaysians still prefer conventional cars due to concerns about charging infrastructure. A 2023 survey revealed that 68% cited limited charging stations as the primary barrier.

What is the main reason Malaysians hesitate to buy electric vehicles?

A is correct: "limited charging stations as the primary barrier" → paraphrased as "insufficient charging stations." B and C are not mentioned — classic distractors.
