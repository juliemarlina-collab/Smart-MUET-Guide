Tips & Tricks
Question-First Strategy
▾
QUESTION-FIRST STRATEGY

Always read the questions first before reading the passage. Underline keywords in each question. Then scan the passage for those keywords — do not read every word.

Identify the Question Type First

Direct retrieval: Answer is copied almost word-for-word from the text.
Paraphrase: Answer is the same meaning as the text, but different words.
Inference: Answer is implied by the text — you need to read between the lines.
AVOID THIS MISTAKE

Do not choose an option just because the words appear in the passage. Distractors often use the same words with different meanings. Always check if the idea matches, not just the words.

Sample Passage Extract

Despite the increasing availability of electric vehicles, many Malaysians still prefer conventional cars due to concerns about charging infrastructure. A 2023 survey revealed that 68% of respondents cited limited charging stations as the primary barrier to adoption.

Sample Question

What is the main reason Malaysians hesitate to buy electric vehicles?

Why A is correct: The passage states "limited charging stations as the primary barrier" — this directly maps to "insufficient charging stations." This is a paraphrase question.

Why B and C are wrong: Neither cost nor awareness is mentioned in this extract. Options that are generally true but not stated in the passage are distractors.
