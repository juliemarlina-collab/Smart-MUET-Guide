Tips & Tricks
Question-First Strategy
▾
QUESTION-FIRST STRATEGY

Always read the questions first before reading the passage. Underline keywords in each question. Then scan the passage for those keywords — do not read every word.

Identify the Question Type First

Direct retrieval: Answer is copied almost word-for-word from the text.
Paraphrase: Answer is the same meaning as the text, but different words.
Inference: Answer is implied by the text — you need to read between the lines.
AVOID THIS MISTAKE

Do not choose an option just because the words appear in the passage. Distractors often use the same words with different meanings. Always check if the idea matches, not just the words.

Sample — Part 1 (Matching)

Read the three place descriptions, then answer: Which place has the cheapest wi-fi?

Place A: Wi-fi RM10/day  |  Place B: Wi-fi RM20/day  |  Place C: Wi-fi RM20/day

Part 1 — Matching (Direct Retrieval)

Which place has the cheapest wi-fi?

Answer: A — Place A.
Place A charges RM10/day — the lowest. Both B and C charge RM20/day. This is direct retrieval — the exact figure appears in the text. Scan for the keyword "wi-fi" and compare the numbers across all three places before answering.
