HEAR technique

Run through these four steps before and during every audio clip — HEAR keeps you listening for evidence instead of guessing from memory.

HHighlight keywordsMark the keywords in each question before its audio starts.
EExpect answer typePredict what kind of answer is needed — a name, number, reason or place.
AAttend to cluesListen for signal words like "however" or "actually" that correct earlier information.
RReview evidenceCheck what you heard against each option before you commit to an answer.
