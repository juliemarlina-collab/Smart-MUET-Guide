Tips & Tricks
Keyword and distractor strategy
▾
KEYWORD STRATEGY

Write 2–3 keywords per blank — not full sentences. If you miss a word, leave it and move on. Complete notes after audio ends.

DISTRACTOR WATCH

First option mentioned is often corrected by the speaker. Listen for: but · however · actually · in fact · no, I mean. The correction = the answer.

Good vs Poor Notes

❌ "The meeting was moved from Thursday to Friday at 2:30 in the science block"
✅ "Fri / 2.30pm / sci block"

Sample & Transcript
See a distractor in action
▾
"Good morning students. The library will be closed this Thursday for maintenance. Actually — sorry, I made an error. The library will be closed on Friday, not Thursday."

When will the library be closed?

B — Friday. Speaker first says Thursday (distractor), then corrects to Friday. Signal: "Actually — sorry, I made an error."
