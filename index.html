MUET Quick Intro
  • MUET is the Malaysian University English Test administered by MPM.
  • It assesses 4 components: Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing.
  • Widely used for university entry and academic English readiness in Malaysia.
›

MUET is administered by MPM (Lembaga Peperiksaan Malaysia). Your result is valid for 5 years. Sessions available: Session 1, 2, 3 and MUET on Demand (MoD).

Why Take MUET?
  • Required or useful for admission to many Malaysian higher education programmes.
  • Helps show English proficiency for study and future career opportunities.
  • Builds confidence in academic communication.
›

Many public and private universities require a minimum MUET band for degree entry. Band 3 or 4 is commonly required. A higher band strengthens scholarship and postgraduate applications.

Important MUET Facts
  • Result validity: 5 years.
  • 2026: Session 1, Session 2, Session 3, and MUET on Demand (MoD).
  • Total score: 360 marks. Band scale: Band 1 to Band 5+.
  • Best to check MPM for the latest official dates.
